the bootloader chain of [[cyber]]. launched november 5, 2021; halted at height 25,120,712 on august 5, 2026. it ran almost 5 years and taught 3.1M [[particles]], 2.9M [[cyberlinks]] by its neurons with 47,837 moon passports. the knowledge moves to [[soft3]].

[bostrom.network](https://bostrom.network) serves the final snapshot: every dataset content-addressed on IPFS, a balance checker (address, mnemonic, private key, ledger), and the swap value of every account in $BOOT at warp prices at halt.

`snapshot/` holds the extraction scripts, the manifest with CID and sha256 of every file, and the dashboard app. everything is reproducible from an archive node.

this repository also carries the bostrom pages of the cyber knowledge graph: architecture, tokenomics, guides, heroes.
